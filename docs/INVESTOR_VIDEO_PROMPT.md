# Investor Video Generation Prompt

To recreate or iterate on the Vertex Sentinel investor video, use the following prompt template. This is designed for high-end AI video generators (e.g., Sora, Runway Gen-3, Luma Dream Machine) or as a creative brief for a production team.

## System Prompt / Creative Brief

**Title:** Vertex Sentinel: The Glass Box Evolution
**Style:** Cinematic Tech Noir / Industrial Minimalism
**Visual Theme:** "Glassmorphism" UI, deep navy and neon cyan accents, translucent layers, high-speed data flow.

### Part 1: The Narrative Arc
"Generate a 60-second high-fidelity cinematic video for a Fintech venture capital pitch. The theme is 'The Evolution of Trust in AI Trading.'

**Opening Scene (0:00-0:10):**
Start with a dark, opaque 'Black Box' floating in a void of chaotic data. The narration (or text overlay) highlights the danger of unsupervised AI trading. Transition the box from opaque to translucent glass, revealing a glowing 'heart' of verified logic.

**Core Technical Showcase (0:10-0:40):**
Display a high-resolution, futuristic 'Professional Risk Terminal.' The UI should feature:
1.  **The Pulse:** A symbolic 'Economic Heartbeat' visualization showing a 0.001 USDC nanopayment moving along a blockchain rail (Arc L1).
2.  **The Signature:** Abstract cryptographic hashes (EIP-712) flying from the terminal and being 'etched' into an immutable glass ledger (The Technical Audit).
3.  **The Guardrail:** A human operator's hand adjusting a translucent neon slider labeled 'Max Position Size,' showing the AI immediately recalibrating its strategy.

**The Performance Proof (0:40-0:50):**
Zoom in on a metrics card showing a **+33.85% ROI** with a smooth, upward-trending green equity curve. The background should show 'Kraken' and 'Binance' exchange logos being processed in parallel.

**Closing (0:50-1:00):**
End with the Vertex Sentinel shield logo. Text: 'VERIFY EVERYTHING. TRUST NOTHING.' Followed by 'Powered by Arc L1 & Genkit AI.'

### Part 2: Visual Style Directives (For AI Generators)
- **Lighting:** Moody, volumetric lighting. Neon cyan glows reflecting off glass surfaces.
- **Camera Work:** Slow, sweeping macro shots of UI elements. High-speed 'data-moshing' transitions between technical layers.
- **Texture:** Brushed metal, frosted glass, digital particles.
- **Resolution:** 4K, 60fps, photorealistic textures."
